Timing confidence & references

ROC REFERENCE Published rounded VCP volume times are used where available for the selected VCP and 6.4° termination, and can be overridden. MODELED Per-elevation scan durations, turnarounds, timing of supplemental scans, and resulting comparisons are educational estimates — not exact ROC scan-estimator results. For other VCPs/terminations, baseline durations are additionally approximate. ROC estimates roughly 20 seconds per added MPDA scan and 1 minute per MPDA SAILS scan; these modeled overheads are not exact. VCP 112 includes MPDA in its baseline. Actual AVSET is adaptive and is not a fixed operator-selected termination angle; this control represents a simulation of a selected cutoff.
